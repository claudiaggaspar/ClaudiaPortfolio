**Goal:** Predict “healthy vs diseased” outcomes from survey data with a real target variable.  
**Tools:** Python, scikit-learn, statsmodels, matplotlib.  

**Key insights:**
- Used Logistic Regression and Random Forest classifiers.  
- Examined multivariate effects using permutation importance.  
- Top features included insulin, heart rate, and blood pressure.

**Performance:**  
ROC-AUC ≈ 0.50, suggesting weak separability in the dataset.
